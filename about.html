About Saloni

A manufacturer focused on forage and livestock equipment.

Saloni Machinery — Henan Saloni Machinery Equipment Co., Ltd — has spent more than 15 years building agricultural machinery in Henan, China. Our range covers the full forage and feeding line: harvester headers, wheel and crawler forage harvesters, TMR mixer feeders, feeding trucks, balers and wrappers, monorail transporters, manure spreaders and field equipment.

We sell directly to farms, dealers and distributors across Europe, the Americas, Southeast Asia, South America, Australia and Africa. Machines are built and inspected in our factory, packed for export, and shipped with the documents your customs clearance needs.

CE certification is available for selected models — ask our team for the certificate covering the machine you need.
