Manufacturing

From our factory floor to your farm

Every Saloni machine is built, assembled and checked before it leaves for the port. Steel frames, gearboxes and cutting parts are inspected during production, and finished machines are test-run so the unit you receive is the unit that was verified.

  • Production and assembly under one roof, with in-process inspection
  • Pre-shipment checks and test runs before packing
  • Export packing suited to long-distance sea transport
  • Wear parts and technical support after delivery

Four things we do for every buyer

01

Application matching
