Sugarcane Harvester

Single-row cane harvesters in crawler and wheeled versions, cutting and windrowing in one pass.

Saloni sugarcane harvesters cut a single row of cane and lay it in a windrow for collection. The crawler 4GP-1 works soft and wet fields, while the wheeled SH15 covers firm ground fast with a double-disc cutter.

Row spacing of 0.9 m and above suits standard cane planting, and the single-cylinder diesel engine on the 4GP-1 keeps fuel and maintenance simple.
