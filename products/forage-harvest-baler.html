Forage Harvester Baler Combo

Harvest and bale in one pass — cut forage goes straight into bales with no second machine.

This combination machine harvests forage and presses it into bales in a single pass, cutting out the separate baling step. Fewer passes mean less fuel, less time and less compaction on the field.
