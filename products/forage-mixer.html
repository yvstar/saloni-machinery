Straw Crusher Mixer

Stationary crusher-mixers that chop straw and blend feed in sizes from 1.5 to 8 m³.

The Saloni straw crusher mixer chops straw, pasture and hay and blends them into a uniform mix for feeding. Electric motor drive keeps running costs low and operation simple — no engine to fuel or service.

Sizes run from 1.5 m³ for small farms to 8 m³ for larger operations, with weighing systems fitted on several models so every batch matches the recipe.
