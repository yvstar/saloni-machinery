Round Baler

Tractor-mounted round balers with a 1,300 mm working width, with a shredder version for straw.

1,300 mm width50–60 hp tractorShredder version

The Saloni round baler picks up windrows and presses them into firm round bales behind your tractor. The 1,300 mm pickup handles wide windrows, and a 50–60 hp tractor is all the power it needs.

A shredder version is available for straw and stubble work, chopping residue as it bales.
