3-Wheel Vertical Mixer Feeder

A self-propelled three-wheel mixer feeder that mixes and delivers feed with a tight turning circle.

This self-propelled three-wheeler carries its own mixing tank, so one operator can mix and feed without a tractor. The three-wheel layout turns tightly at the end of feed alleys and in small yards.

The vertical mixing system handles silage, straw and concentrates, with a 4–5 m³ box sized for daily feeding rounds.
