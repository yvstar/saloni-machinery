Square Baler

The Saloni square baler presses hay and straw into dense rectangular bales that stack neatly for transport and storage. The 2.2 m pickup covers a wide windrow in each pass.

A 50 hp tractor powers the machine, and the 1,640 kg frame is built for season after season of baling work.
