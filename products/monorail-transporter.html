Monorail Transporter

Rail transporters that carry 200–800 kg up steep orchard slopes on a single track.

The Saloni monorail transporter runs on a single steel rail laid up hillsides, carrying fruit, fertilizer and tools where no road or truck can go. It climbs steep grades that defeat wheeled vehicles.

Payloads run from 200 to 800 kg, with gasoline and diesel engine options and a dual-track version for heavier loads. The rail follows the terrain, so installation needs no grading or concrete.
