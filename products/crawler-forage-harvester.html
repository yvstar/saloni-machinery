Crawler Forage Harvester

Tracked forage harvesters that keep working on wet and soft ground where wheels bog down.

Where fields are wet, soft or hilly, wheels stop and tracks keep going. Saloni crawler forage harvesters run on steel tracks with low ground pressure, so harvest continues in conditions that would strand a wheeled machine.

The range covers 80, 100 and 120 hp class machines, plus a larger 4QZL-8 model. The 80 hp model cuts a 1,400 mm pass, and a cab version is available for long shifts.
