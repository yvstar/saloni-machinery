Manure Spreader

Tractor-trailed spreaders from 1.8 to 10 m³ that turn manure into even, field-ready fertiliser.

Saloni manure spreaders take solid manure from the yard to the field and spread it in an even layer. The range runs from a compact 1.8 m³ unit for small farms to 10 m³ machines for large operations.

The trailed design works with the tractor you already own, and the spreading mechanism lays an even pattern across the working width.
