3-Wheel Feeding Truck

Three-wheel feeding trucks in diesel and battery-electric versions for fast, quiet feeding rounds.

3 m³ boxDiesel & electric3-wheel self-propelled

Tell us your tractor power, working conditions and destination port for an accurate quote and configuration.

The Saloni 3-wheel feeding truck carries a 3 m³ box of mixed feed along the fence line and discharges it evenly as it drives. One operator feeds a whole barn in a single pass.

Choose the 25 hp diesel version for long rounds, or the battery-electric version for quiet indoor work with no exhaust in the barn.
