Self-Propelled Wheeled Forage Harvester

Saloni wheel forage harvesters combine cutting, chopping and blowing in one self-propelled machine. The 1,800 mm header cuts a wide pass, and the chopping drum delivers an even chop for quality silage.

Models cover the 140 hp class, including a four-wheel-drive version with an air-conditioned cab for long days in the field. The high unloading spout fills trailers directly.
