Remote Control Mower

A remote-controlled mower with an 800 mm cut for slopes up to 45°.

The HT-80S remote control mower cuts grass and brush on slopes where walking behind a mower is dangerous. The operator stands clear and drives the machine by remote control, up to 45° of slope.

An 800 mm cutting width and adjustable stubble height from 20 to 200 mm handle orchard rows, roadside verges and rough ground. Travel speed runs from 0 to 3.6 km/h for precise work around trees.
