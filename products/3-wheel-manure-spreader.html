3-Wheel Manure Spreader

A self-propelled three-wheel spreader with a 1.5 m³ tank for yards, barns and small fields.

This self-propelled three-wheel spreader carries its own 1.5 m³ tank, so spreading does not tie up a tractor. The compact 4.48 m body turns in yards and small fields, and the 1,300 kg machine is light on soft ground.

The engine powers both driving and spreading, controlled from the driving position.
