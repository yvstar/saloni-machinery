Cattle Hoof Trimming Truck

A vertical hoof-trimming truck with hydraulic lifting that holds cattle safely for trimming.

Hydraulic operation3 kW powerVertical chute

The Saloni hoof trimming truck lifts and holds cattle in a vertical chute so hooves can be trimmed safely and quickly. Hydraulic power lifts the body and each hoof in turn, with the animal held calm and secure.

The unit runs on 3 kW hydraulic power and is built for regular trimming rounds on dairy and beef farms.
