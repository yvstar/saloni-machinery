Forage Harvesting

Forage Harvester Header

A side-mounted header that cuts and feeds maize and grass into the harvester at up to 34 t/h.

The Saloni forage harvester header cuts and gathers forage crops in one pass. Twelve cutting blades and four gathering drums feed the crop smoothly into the harvester, with 24 cut-length settings from 2 to 36 mm to match the chop your silage needs.

Power comes from the tractor PTO — 55 to 95 hp at 540 or 1,000 rpm — through a gearbox and pulley transmission. An optional support wheel is available for uneven fields.
