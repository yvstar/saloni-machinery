Tractor-Trailed Vertical TMR Mixer

Trailed vertical TMR mixers in 5 and 9 m³, powered by your tractor for even rations every day.

5 & 9 m³ modelsTractor-trailedVertical auger

This trailed vertical mixer uses your tractor's power to chop and blend forage, silage and concentrates into a uniform total mixed ration. The vertical auger mixes gently without over-processing fibre.

Two sizes — 5 m³ and 9 m³ — cover small and large herds. The mixing tank uses a 20 mm bottom plate on the 9 m³ model for long wear life.
