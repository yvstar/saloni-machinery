Silage Reclaimer

A 4 m electric reclaimer that mills a clean silage face and loads feed without waste.

4 m face widthElectric drive1,100 kg

The Saloni silage reclaimer mills silage from the bunker face in a clean, even cut, leaving a smooth face that stays fresh and loading feed with minimal losses. A ragged face lets air in and spoils feed — a clean face keeps it.

Electric motors drive the picking drum and travel, with 220/380 V supply, and the 4.3 m machine works along standard bunker widths.
