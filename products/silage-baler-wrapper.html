Silage Baler Wrapper

Bales and wraps silage in one machine — hay, straw and forage sealed for fermentation.

The Saloni silage baler wrapper presses forage into firm round bales and wraps them in stretch film in the same cycle. Sealed bales ferment into high-quality silage without a bunker or clamp.

Diesel and electric versions are available, so the machine fits farms with or without three-phase power. Bale size and wrapping layers are set to your crop and storage needs.
