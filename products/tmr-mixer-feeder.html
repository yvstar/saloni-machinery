TMR Mixer Feeder Wagon

A 5 m³ mixer wagon that chops, mixes and feeds total mixed ration to cattle, horses and sheep.

The Saloni TMR mixer feeder wagon chops straw and forage, mixes the ration evenly and discharges it along the feed fence — one machine for the whole feeding round.

The 5 m³ box suits medium herds, and the compact body is built for barn alleys and feed fences.
